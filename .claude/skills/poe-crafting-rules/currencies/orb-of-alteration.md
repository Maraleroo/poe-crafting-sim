**Orb of Alteration**
- Applies to: magic items.
- Not applicable when: the item is not magic. The reason shown is the game's message "Failed to apply item: Target is not Magic."
- Effect: all prefixes and suffixes of the item are replaced by new ones, rolled exactly as with the Orb of Transmutation, rules 1 to 3. By chance the new mods can be the same as the old ones.
  1. The item gets 1 or 2 mods, each with a 50% chance.
  2. One mod: it is chosen from one pool of all valid prefixes and all valid suffixes together. The chance of a mod is its weight divided by the sum of the weights of all mods in this pool.
  3. Two mods: one prefix is chosen from the valid prefixes and one suffix from the valid suffixes. Within each pool, the chance of a mod is its weight divided by the sum of the weights in that pool. If one type has no valid mod, that mod is left out; no second mod of the other type is chosen instead.
  4. If there is no valid mod at all, applying the orb fails with the game's message "Failed to apply item: Could not generate Mod." and the item stays unchanged.
- Evidence: measured by the PO on 2026-10-21 with 2000 Orbs of Alteration on a Bone Ring (item level 83): 2 mods 50.6 % (±2.2 %); with one mod, 70.6 % suffixes against 69.4 % expected from the weights; a 50:50 choice between prefix and suffix is ruled out (z ≈ 13); the frequencies of all 40 mod groups match their weights. The replacement of the old mods, the not-magic case and both messages are known from the game. The empty-pool case in rule 3 is the PO's assumption and not measured. Not documented by GGG.
