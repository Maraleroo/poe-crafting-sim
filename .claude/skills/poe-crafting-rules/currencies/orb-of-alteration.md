**Orb of Alteration**
- Applies to: magic items.
- Not applicable when: the item is not magic. The reason shown is the game's message "Failed to apply item: Target is not Magic."
- Effect: all prefixes and suffixes of the item are removed; then new mods are added as described below. By chance the new mods can be the same as the old ones.
  The orb chooses a target number of mods: 1 or 2, each with a 50 % chance. It then adds random mods until the item has that many mods or no mod is added. The orb always takes effect, also when it adds no mod; the item can become magic with no mods at all. These orbs never show "Failed to apply item: Could not generate Mod." Evidence: Focused Amulet (magic limits 0 prefixes and 0 suffixes), tested by the PO on 2026-10-31: Transmutation and Alteration each gave a magic item with no mods and no message. For an item with at most 1 prefix and 1 suffix, this rule gives the same chances as the old rules 2 and 3, so the Bone Ring measurements still hold.
- Evidence: measured by the PO on 2026-10-21 with 2000 Orbs of Alteration on a Bone Ring (item level 83): 2 mods 50.6 % (±2.2 %); with one mod, 70.6 % suffixes against 69.4 % expected from the weights; a 50:50 choice between prefix and suffix is ruled out (z ≈ 13); the frequencies of all 40 mod groups match their weights. The replacement of the old mods, the not-magic case and both messages are known from the game. Not documented by GGG.
