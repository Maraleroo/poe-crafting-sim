**Regal Orb**
- Applies to: magic items.
- Not applicable when: the item is not magic. The reason shown is the game's message "Failed to apply item: Target is not Magic."
- Not applicable when: the item is magic and neither side has room under the affix limits of a rare item. The reason shown is the game's message "Failed to apply item: Item has no space for more Mods."
- Effect: the item becomes rare. Its mods stay unchanged, including their values, and one random mod is added as described in "Adding a random mod", under the affix limits of a rare item. If the valid pool is empty, no mod is added, the item does not change and the reason shown is the game's message "Failed to apply item: Could not generate Mod."
- Evidence: tested by the PO on 2026-11-01. A normal item and a rare item: "Failed to apply item: Target is not Magic.". A magic Composite Ring with 2 prefixes ("Incandescent" Tier 2, "Dragon's" Tier 2) became rare with both mods and their values unchanged and the new prefix "Glinting" (Tier 6). A magic Focused Amulet with no mods (limits as a magic item: 0 prefixes and 0 suffixes; "-1 Prefix Modifier allowed", "-2 Suffix Modifiers allowed") became rare with the suffix "of the Furnace". The two cases without a new mod are a PO rule by analogy with the Orb of Augmentation, not tested: the PO found no item where they occur (tried, among others, a magic level-1 sword with "Cannot roll Attack Modifiers"; free suffixes remained). With the ring bases in the data they cannot occur.
