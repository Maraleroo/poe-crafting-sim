**Orb of Scouring**
- Applies to: magic and rare items.
- Not applicable when: the item is normal or unique. The reason shown is the game's message "Failed to apply item: Target is not Magic or Rare."
- Effect: all prefixes and suffixes of the item are removed. The item becomes normal and loses its name. Implicit mods, base and item level stay unchanged. The level requirement is then that of a normal item of the base.
- Not modelled yet: mods protected by "Prefixes Cannot Be Changed" or "Suffixes Cannot Be Changed", and fractured mods, stay on the item; the rarity then depends on the remaining mods. Until such mods exist, scouring always gives a normal item.
- Evidence: tested by the PO on 2026-11-02: a normal item and a unique item: "Failed to apply item: Target is not Magic or Rare."; a rare Iron Ring "Blight Finger" (item level 83, 3 prefixes, 3 suffixes, level requirement 26) and a magic "Dancer's Iron Ring of Victory" (item level 83, level requirement 18) both became a normal "Iron Ring" with item level 83, its implicit "Adds 1 to 4 Physical Damage to Attacks" and no requirements.
